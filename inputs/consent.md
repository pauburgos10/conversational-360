# Before you start

## What this is
You have been asked to give 360° feedback on a colleague as part of Pluralit's
half-yearly development review. This is a guided conversation rather than a form:
you will rate a set of competencies and be asked a few open questions, with
occasional follow-ups to make your input concrete enough to be useful.

**Estimated time: 12–18 minutes.** You can stop at any point and resume from the
same link — your progress is saved as you go.

## Who will see what you write

**Please read this section before you start. It changed for this round.**

| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| **HR / the review administrators** | Your answers **as you wrote them**, with **your name against them**. |
| **The colleague you are reviewing** | A version with names and contact details removed, **not attributed to you**, with open answers paraphrased into themes rather than quoted. |
| **Their manager, in the development conversation** | The same version the colleague gets. |

So: **your feedback is confidential from the person you are reviewing. It is not
anonymous to HR.**

This is a deliberate change. HR runs the review and needs to see who contributed
and what was said in order to administer the round, follow up on serious concerns,
and stand behind the process. If you were told in a previous round that nobody
could ever look up what you said, that is no longer accurate, and we would rather
tell you plainly than let you assume it.

**Write accordingly.** Say what is true and useful, and say it as something you
would be willing to stand behind in a conversation with HR.

## What happens to your answers

- Your open answers are stored **as written** and are visible to HR alongside
  your name.
- Before anything reaches the colleague being reviewed, names, contact details,
  ticket references and phrases that would identify you by role or position are
  **removed automatically**, and themes are **paraphrased, never quoted**.
- **No breakdown by role.** No report shows whether feedback came from a manager,
  a peer, or a direct report — not even the HR copy.
- Numeric results are shown as aggregates, and figures built from too few
  responses are withheld rather than published.

## Please read this part carefully

Confidential from the subject is not the same as unidentifiable to them.

This round runs with a small number of reviewers per person — often only two or
three. When an average is built from that few responses, **the person receiving
the feedback may be able to work out who said what**, simply from who they know
was asked. The system does not tell them, and their copy carries no attribution;
but arithmetic and context can.

If there is something you do not feel able to raise this way, please raise it
with HR directly instead.

## What it is not used for
This feedback supports a development conversation. It does **not** produce an
automated performance score, a seniority level, a promotion decision, or a
salary band position. Those decisions are made by people, not by this system.

Your own self-assessment, if you are also being reviewed, is kept separate and
is **not counted** in anyone's average. It is shown only as a point of
comparison in your development conversation.

## Retention
Raw responses, including your name against them, are deleted after 12 months.
Aggregated, de-identified results are retained.
