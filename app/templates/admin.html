Which kind of question a competency gets

Once every item in a competency is answered, its ratings are checked against these rules, top to bottom. The first that matches decides the question.

  1. Not observed. The reviewer marked at least one item as something they have not seen. They get an optional question asking whether they noticed anything anyway — never a follow-up.
  2. Low. The average rating is {{ policy_rules.low }} or below. The question asks what that looks like in practice.
  3. Mixed. The items within the competency differ by {{ policy_rules.mixed }} point{{ '' if policy_rules.mixed == 1 else 's' }} or more. The question asks which part holds up and which slips.
  4. High. The average rating is {{ policy_rules.high }} or above. The question asks for an example of it making a difference.
  5. General. None of the above. Competencies are decided in order without looking ahead, so a general question takes a slot only when the competencies still to come could not fill the remaining slots anyway — it can never use up a slot a later competency with a signal would need.

Two further rules stop a dimension from collecting ratings only. A competency with no signal keeps its slot when it is the last chance for its dimension — which is why Delivery, the sole competency in its own dimension, always earns a question. And the final slot is held for a dimension that has nothing yet, while a competency belonging to it is still to come.

These rules are fixed in the engine rather than editable here, and the wording of each question is set in inputs/questions.json, which HR and Legal review.
