What this page can and cannot tell you

not opened also covers a reviewer who opened the link and declined — consent.md promises that declining records nothing, so this page must not let you tell the two apart. in progress means consent was given and the interview is unfinished; it is read from the checkpoint, since no row reaches responses.db until submission.

Started without an invite rows are reviews begun from Start a review rather than from a minted link. They are listed while in progress so a reviewer who lost the tab can be sent their link to carry on. One that has not got past the consent screen, or was declined, is not listed, for the same reason as above.

An assigned address makes that reviewer's answers attributable to them on the HR copy of the subject's report. That is intended — HR administers the round — and reviewers are told so on the consent screen before they write anything. The subject copy of the same report masks names and contact details and attributes nothing.

Answer content is deliberately kept off this page. It belongs on a report somebody opened on purpose, not on the list HR leaves open while chasing responses.
